Sabrina's Balai

Sabrina’s Balai is a web-based reservation system designed to simplify resort bookings through an intuitive online platform. Customers can check availability, reserve preferred dates, and choose payment options with ease, while administrators receive automated email notifications containing booking schedules and customer details. The system is built using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Google Sheets, and Google Apps Script for efficient and accessible data management.
